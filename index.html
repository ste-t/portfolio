04 / PROFILE

Curiosity, made
practical.

I'm a computer engineering student interested in what happens between the layers.

My work spans software, Linux infrastructure, hardware, and technical investigation, with a particular interest in cybersecurity, privacy, and understanding how systems work beneath the surface. I enjoy exploring systems from the hardware and firmware layers up to the applications people use, then applying what I learn to build practical solutions.

Beyond software, I work with electronics, CAD, 3D printing, and hardware repair, including component-level troubleshooting and microsoldering. I value hands-on experimentation, careful documentation, and understanding a problem before trying to solve it.
